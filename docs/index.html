How to Install JZS Brawl on Android

Installing JZS Brawl on Android is easy and only takes a few steps. If it’s your first time, follow the guide below:

Step-by-step Guide

  1. Download the JZS Brawl APK with the Download button on this page.
  2. Open your file manager or browser, go to the Downloads folder and tap the JZS Brawl APK.
  3. If it’s your first time, your phone shows a warning saying it isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source.
  4. Tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source”.
  5. Go back and the install window opens. Tap Install and wait a few seconds.
  6. Done! Open JZS Brawl and enjoy brawling.

How to Play JZS Brawl on PC

To play JZS Brawl on PC you need the APK and an Android emulator like BlueStacks. Once you have both, follow these steps:

First Method

  1. Download the JZS Brawl APK on your computer.
  2. Download and install the BlueStacks emulator on your PC.
  3. Launch BlueStacks.
  4. In the BlueStacks side bar, click the “Install APK” button.
  5. A window opens: go to the folder where the APK is saved.
  6. Select the APK and click Open.
  7. BlueStacks installs the game.
  8. When it’s done, the JZS Brawl icon shows up on the BlueStacks home screen. Click it to play.

Second Method

Even faster: after launching BlueStacks (steps 1 to 3 above), just drag and drop the APK into the BlueStacks window. The install starts on its own. Once it’s done, open the game and play.

How to Log In With Your Supercell ID

To play JZS Brawl with your account, log in with your Supercell ID:

  1. Install and open JZS Brawl.
  2. During the tutorial, tap the Supercell ID button in the top-left corner of the screen.
  3. Enter the email linked to your Supercell ID and tap Next.
  4. Enter the verification code you received by email and tap Next.
  5. Your Brawl Stars account shows up. Tap Done.
  6. That’s it! Your account is loaded in JZS Brawl.
